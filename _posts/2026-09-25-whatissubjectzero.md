title: "What is Subject Zero?"
author: bread
---

Subject Zero is going to be an incremental platformer, initially for mobile, but we hope to expand it to Steam and Itch.io later. You are a monster, locked away by the government for testing. One day, there is an earthquake, and you are released from your glass prison. Now, you must run, crawl, and fight your way out, before the government agents are able to take care of you, permanently.
